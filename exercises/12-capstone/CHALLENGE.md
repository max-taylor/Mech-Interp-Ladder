# 12 — Capstone mini-project
**Challenge:** Spend about a week on one small open question. Fill in `WRITEUP.md` (1 page). Suggestions:
1. **Feature absorption:** do first-letter features in a GPT-2 SAE fail on tokens a more specific feature absorbs? (Chanin et al. 2024)
2. **SAEs vs probes:** on exercise 09's task, does a probe on SAE latents match the dense probe? (DeepMind's 2025 negative SAE results)
3. **Transcoders for IOI:** can transcoder features trace the IOI circuit more cleanly than heads? (Dunefsky et al. 2024)
4. **Attribution graphs:** trace one GPT-2 prompt with the open-source circuit-tracer library. (Anthropic 2025)
5. **Backup heads:** what triggers backup name movers? (McGrath et al. 2023, "Hydra effect")
**Done when:** `WRITEUP.md` is filled in. No automated check.
**Blind spots to ask an LLM about:** scoping, baselines, controls, negative results.
**Stretch:** Share it publicly.
